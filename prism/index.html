PRISM

Pragyan Responsible Intelligence
& Solution Methodology.

A structured path from understanding the business to building, deploying and improving the right AI solution.

Five phases.
Fourteen steps.

PRISM keeps the technology decision in the right place: after the business, processes, data and opportunities are understood.

ORIENT

1. Understand the Business & Set Goals

Business goals, key challenges, stakeholder inputs and success measures.

2. Map the Current Setup

Processes, systems, data sources, connections and current gaps.

DISPERSE

3. Find Where AI Can Help

Process improvement areas, priority use cases and AI opportunities.

4. Check the Data & Knowledge

Data sources, quality, knowledge sources and gaps.

5. Check Enterprise Readiness

Technology, security, people, skills and governance readiness.

SPECTRUM

6. Identify the Best Opportunities

Compare use cases by business impact and priority.

7. Business Value Engineering

Validate the business case, value, investment and operational impact.

REFRACT

8. Define What the Solution Needs

Translate the business problem into functional and AI requirements.

9. Design the Solution

RAG, fine-tuning, knowledge graphs, agents, copilots, MCP and integrations.

10. Choose the Right Technology

Benchmark models, platforms, hosting, cost and scalability.

EMERGE

11. Proof of Concept

Build, test with users and measure expected value.

12. Build the Solution

Engineer, integrate, test and prepare for production.

13. Launch & Manage

Deploy with monitoring, governance, training and support.

14. Measure & Improve

Review business outcomes, AI performance and next opportunities.
